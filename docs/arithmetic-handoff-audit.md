## First quantitative statements and their quantifiers

**Theta branch.** The first explicit analytic inequality in the theta comparison type is `Decay`. The outer quantifiers run over number fields $F,E$ with the given algebra and endomorphism $\sigma$, base $B$, ranks $a,a'\in\mathbb N$, geometry $G$, character data and Weil representations satisfying `IsTriple`. The guards include the CM extension conditions, $a'<a$, even $a+a'$, and geometric and character restrictions. Every Schwartz $\Phi$ on the first side satisfies convergence. For each $\Phi'$ in `G.M'.So B.ψ B.τ₁` and each $N\in\mathbb N$, there exist $C\in\mathbb R$ and $k\in\mathbb N$ such that for all $g$ in `UA σ G.V'.J` and $h$ in `UA σ G.W.J`,

$$
 \|\theta_{\mathrm{om}'}(\Phi',g,h)\|\,\operatorname{qht}(g)^N
 \le C\,\operatorname{ht}(h)^k.
$$

Here `ht` is `heightGL` on the adelic group and `qht` takes its infimum over rational left translates. The type also requires convergence and moderate growth of the second integral. The witnesses come after the triple, test function and $N$, and before $g,h$. They may depend on the fields, ranks, representation and character data. The type provides no explicit dependence on conductor or level and contains no scale $X$ or iteration length $m$. The Lean variable `X` in `IsTriple` denotes character data.
